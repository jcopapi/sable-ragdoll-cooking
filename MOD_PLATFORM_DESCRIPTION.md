# Sable Ragdoll: Cooking

Turn fallen mobs into physical carcasses, cook them over a campfire, and harvest extra loot. Fatal hits use **Ragdoll Reactions** physics, while the mob's original drops are saved so cooking never rerolls its loot table.

## Features

- Cook carcasses over lit campfires or soul campfires for more food and loot.
- Carry bodies or move them with a lead; cooking progress is saved.
- Built-in recipes for vanilla mobs, with datapack and optional KubeJS customization.
- Optional Farmer's Delight knives and meat cuts, blood particles, and fuel-burning campfires.
- Nonfatal mob ragdolls are disabled by default; this can be changed in the server config.

## How to play

Kill a supported mob, move its ragdoll near a lit campfire, then harvest the cooked body with a sword. With Farmer's Delight installed, use a knife instead. Harvest a raw body to recover its original drops.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge, and Ragdoll Reactions 0.7.0. Ragdoll Reactions supplies its own dependencies. Other integrations are optional.

For configuration and recipe examples, see the [project documentation](https://github.com/jcopapi/sable-ragdoll-cooking#readme).
